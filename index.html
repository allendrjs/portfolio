Budget Tracker

A personal finance app — expense tracking, budgets, savings goals and recurring transactions, with six months of seeded history so a demo account opens with something to look at. React and TypeScript on the front, a Laravel 12 REST API and MySQL behind it, the whole thing brought up with Docker Compose.

Money is stored as integer minor units rather than floats, so rounding can’t drift. Edits to a past transaction revert and re-apply rather than patching a balance in place. 42 endpoints, and 145 backend tests covering them.

NBA Game Outcome & Score Predictor

Random forest models that pick the winner and the final score of an NBA game from team stats available before tipoff — season and last-ten form, home/away splits, strength of schedule, streaks, rest days, head-to-head. Trained on 2,268 games across the 2023–26 seasons.

74.4% accuracy on the winner against a 54.9% baseline, and a mean error of about 9.6 points on the score. The train/test split is chronological rather than random, so the model is never scored on games it could have learned from. Ships as a self-contained HTML dashboard that runs with no server.
